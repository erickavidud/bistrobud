The problem

Restaurants run on bets.

Managers predict demand, inventory, prep, and staffing before the first customer walks in. When those guesses are wrong, the cost appears as waste, overtime, stockouts, and lost time.

Replace guesswork with decisions.

Bistro Bud turns scattered restaurant data into practical next steps before service starts.
